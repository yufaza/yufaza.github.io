Project case study

Otsuka GO

A collaborative employee-platform project at PT Amerta Indah Otsuka, bringing news, information, learning, training, and engagement into one destination.

01

A common destination for the employee experience

Otsuka GO was an all-in-one employee platform at PT Amerta Indah Otsuka. I collaborated with the development team on an application bringing company news, information, learning, training, and entertainment together for employees.

Its purpose was to make the platform useful across the working day: a place to find information, engage with learning, and participate in a shared employee environment.

02

Bring different employee needs into a coherent experience

Information, learning, and engagement serve different purposes, but employees should be able to approach them through a familiar product. The challenge is to make a shared platform feel coherent while preserving the purpose of each area.

For a team-built application, that also makes consistency important: each contribution needs to fit the wider experience rather than behave as a disconnected feature.

03

Product focus

  • Company news and information

    A shared destination for employees to access company information and updates.

  • Learning and training

    Learning-related content sits within the broader employee platform rather than requiring an entirely separate product experience.

  • Engagement and entertainment

    Employee engagement gives the platform a broader purpose than administrative information alone.

  • An integrated employee destination

    The product brings these areas together through a common application experience.

04

Information, learning, and participation in one place

05

My contribution as part of the team

I collaborated with the team creating the Otsuka GO employee application. My contribution belongs to the wider team delivery, rather than sole ownership of the product.

The experience forms part of my enterprise web-development background: translating business needs into application behavior and contributing within a shared platform serving employees across the organization.

06

A product view of the platform

The conceptual architecture places the employee-facing application around company content, learning, and engagement capabilities. The flow above explains how those areas support the employee experience.

This case study focuses on the product purpose and my collaborative contribution, without assigning an unverified technology stack or reproducing the former company’s internal system design.

07

Outcomes & lessons

Otsuka GO represents my experience contributing to a broad employee platform, where usefulness depends on how well multiple kinds of content and activity fit together.

The main lesson is that an employee platform needs a clear reason to return. Information, learning, and engagement can reinforce that purpose when they are delivered as a consistent experience.
